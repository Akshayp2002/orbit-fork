First public release of Orbit: turn the dial on your **CMF Buds Pro 2** case into a controller.

**Download for your device below** (Assets):
- **Windows 10/11:** `Orbit-Windows-Setup.exe`. If Windows says "protected your PC": More info → Run anyway. Say yes to the admin prompt (needed for press-and-hold).
- **macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon):** `Orbit-macOS.zip`. Unzip, move to Applications, right-click → Open the first time, allow Accessibility.
- **Android 8+:** `Orbit-Android.apk`. Install, then follow the in-app checklist.

Double-press the dial to open the wheel, turn to pick, press to choose. Tools: volume, brightness, scroll / reels, slides, video timeline, app switcher, undo / redo, tabs, desktops, camera shutter, your own keyboard shortcuts. Customize everything in Settings.

Known limits: fast spins drop some clicks (the buds' firmware), macOS shows its own volume slider per click, hold isn't available on macOS. See the README for details. Bugs and ideas: open an issue.
